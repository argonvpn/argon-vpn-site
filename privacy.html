Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains what Argon VPN does with information when you use the app.

What we do not collect

We do not record or store the websites you visit, the content of your traffic, your DNS requests or the IP addresses you connect from, and we do not build profiles of our users.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the list of servers, your language and your connection settings on your device only. They are used to make the app work and are removed when you uninstall it.

Connection to our servers

When you open the app it downloads the current server list from our server. For this request our server necessarily sees your IP address, like any website you open. This information is used only to deliver the list.

Your traffic

While the VPN is on, your traffic is encrypted and passes through the VPN server you are connected to. The server operator can technically see the destination of the traffic but we do not keep records of it.

Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties. We may disclose information only where this is required by law.

Advertising

The app currently contains no advertising. If it is added later, this policy will be updated before it starts.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
